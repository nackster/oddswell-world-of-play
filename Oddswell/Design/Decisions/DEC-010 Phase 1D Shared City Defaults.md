# DEC-010 Phase 1D Shared City Defaults

## Decision

The owner approved the smallest Phase 1D shared-city direction:

- prove two local playable clients in one bounded Sundale instance before any hosting choice;
- spend `$0.00` and add no external multiplayer service, account system, matchmaking, friends, chat, or voice;
- players pass through one another during beta instead of physically blocking;
- use temporary visible labels such as `Player 1` and `Player 2`; these are proof identities, not accounts or permanent player names;
- replicate position, rotation, movement state, approved appearance, and equipped clothing through ordered subphases;
- prove leave/reconnect before measuring a larger local concurrency ladder;
- choose a beta instance ceiling only from measured evidence; and
- require a minimum blocking/reporting surface before external beta access.

## Delivery split

Phase 1D.1 proves two local clients, replicated temporary identity, non-blocking player collision, and authoritative movement observation. It does not claim appearance synchronization or reconnect.

Phase 1D.2 will synchronize the selected placeholder appearance and equipped starter clothing. Phase 1D.3 will prove leave/reconnect and visible-state restoration. Capacity measurement and moderation remain later gates.

## Boundary

This decision does not approve external hosting, port forwarding, deployment, online accounts, platform services, regional infrastructure, purchases, Odds Bucks, wagering, item ownership transfer, trading, chat, voice, friends, blocking/reporting implementation, production capacity, or final character/city art.
