# DEC-002: First Playable Scope

## Decision

The first playable 3D scope contains:

- One compact, visually memorable city.
- One customizable apartment.
- One player avatar.
- Two basketball teams.
- One arena or simplified match venue.
- Match-winner predictions.
- A watchable representation of authoritative match event logs.
- One work or recovery activity.
- A small clothing and furniture catalog.

## Guardrail

Do not build a generalized multi-city framework before the first city reveals what actually needs to be reusable.
