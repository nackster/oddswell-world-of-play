# DEC-002: First Playable Scope

## Decision

The first playable 3D scope contains:

- One compact, visually memorable city.
- A fast default-character choice with varied appearances and a minimal worn starter outfit.
- A purchasable six-tier apartment ladder from Studio through Penthouse.
- One player avatar who can walk through the city.
- Shared city presence where players can walk together and see one another's equipped clothing.
- Two basketball teams.
- An 82-game regular-season target per team; schedule variety with two teams remains an owner decision.
- One arena or simplified match venue.
- One physical work location and one physical wagering location.
- Match winner, winning margin, overtime, and player-points wager targets, introduced and validated one at a time.
- A short watchable representation of authoritative match event logs; the initial duration target is two to five minutes.
- One work or allowance recovery activity using Odds Bucks.
- A small clothing and furniture catalog.
- Basic clothing and furniture stores.
- Six apartment tiers: Studio, One-bedroom, Two-bedroom, Three-bedroom, Four-bedroom, and Penthouse.

The complete beta product authority is [[Design/Beta Game Bible|OddsWell Beta Game Bible]]. Open details in that document are not implementation authorization.

## Guardrail

Do not build a generalized multi-city framework before the first city reveals what actually needs to be reusable. Houses, mansions, additional cities, other sports, real-money Odds Bucks purchases, player-to-player item trading, and the full 10-20-year career ecosystem remain outside beta unless the owner changes scope.
