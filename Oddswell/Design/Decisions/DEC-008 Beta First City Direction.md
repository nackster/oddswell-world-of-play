# DEC-008: Beta First City Direction

## Decision

The owner selected **Concept B — Daymark / Sundale** on July 20, 2026 as the canonical design direction for the beta's first city.

The approved direction is a grounded modern-day civic neighborhood organized around a sunlit central commons. The starter studio, job, clothing store, furniture store, public basketball court, arena, and physical sportsbook form a mostly flat, compact walking ring.

**Sundale remains the working city name until the owner explicitly locks or replaces the name.** The selected theme, geography, atmosphere, palette, material family, landmark logic, and compact-loop direction may now guide Phase 1C.1.

## Approved visual language

- Warm limestone, muted terracotta, sand-colored concrete, sage greenery, restrained cobalt, and burnt-orange accents.
- Late-afternoon daylight with patterned shade and a welcoming social atmosphere.
- Stucco, textured concrete, brick pavers, glass storefronts, painted metal awnings, and mature shade trees.
- The commons tree canopy, public court, and arena axis provide navigation without oversized signs.
- Purchased clothing should remain readable against pale walls and natural shade.

## Guardrails

- Keep the city contemporary, human-scale, and distinctive without luxury, futurism, fantasy monumentality, or a resort appearance.
- Use local wear, repeated awning geometry, a distinctive commons shape, and a memorable court treatment to avoid a generic mixed-use-development look.
- Concept imagery is mood and planning evidence, not a final asset, production model, texture license, complete city, or measured traversal proof.
- Rookwell and Alderpoint remain rejected alternatives for this beta city, not deleted ideas or approved later cities.
- Water-focused and Tartaria-inspired monumental cities remain post-beta ideas only.
- Phase 1C.1 may freeze the minimal art-direction sheet and compact walking-loop layout; it may not claim final art, multiplayer, economy, wagering, stores, apartments, or complete 3D implementation.
