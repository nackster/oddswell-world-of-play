# DEC-011 Empty Starter Studio

## Decision

The player's starting Studio apartment is completely empty of furniture and decoration.

- No bed, chair, table, storage object, wall decoration, rug, appliance, or other placeable item is granted for free.
- The playable shell may contain only the structural room, floor, walls, ceiling, entry/exit, collision, and necessary lighting.
- Every visible placeable furnishing must come from a later valid purchase and ownership record.
- Removing starter furniture does not remove the Studio itself; every player still begins with the modest Studio home required by the Beta Game Bible.

This preserves the intended “start from scratch” progression: the empty space makes each later purchase visible and earned.

## Delivery boundary

Phase 1E may prove an empty Studio before the furniture store or Odds Bucks ledger exists. It must not fabricate purchases, balances, free starter inventory, or furniture ownership. Furniture buying belongs to the authorized economy/store phases, after their owner gates are resolved.

Apartment instancing, visits, layout strategy, placement controls, and the exact furniture catalog remain separate open decisions.
