# DEC-012 Phase 1E Apartment Defaults

## Decision

The owner approved the minimum beta apartment rules:

- apartment interiors are private to their owning player;
- visiting another player's apartment is not part of the beta;
- the starting Studio is completely empty under [[Design/Decisions/DEC-011 Empty Starter Studio]]; and
- after a player legitimately purchases furniture, initial placement uses a small set of predefined snap points rather than unrestricted free placement.

The player must buy every placeable item. Phase 1E cannot grant demonstration furniture, starter inventory, or a fabricated purchase merely to populate the room.

## Delivery split

Phase 1E.1 proves one private, empty, walkable Studio and a reversible city transition. Phase 1E.2 may add the smallest durable Studio ownership and return-location record. The six apartment tiers remain progression data until later phases authorize their requirements and interiors.

The fixed placement points become active only after an authorized Odds Bucks ledger, store purchase, and item-ownership path exist. Their exact count and locations remain a later content decision.

## Boundary

This decision does not approve apartment visits, shared interiors, furniture, a catalog, free inventory, Odds Bucks, purchases, prices, refunds, trading, arbitrary placement, final interiors, external hosting, or deployment. The layout relationship between the six apartment tiers remains open.
