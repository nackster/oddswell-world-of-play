# DEC-007: Phase 1B Character Defaults

## Decision

The owner approved these beta defaults on July 19, 2026:

- A third-person trailing camera that keeps the equipped clothing visible.
- Keyboard/mouse and controller movement with walk, run, and jump.
- No beta combat, crouching, parkour, or vehicles.
- Eight gameplay-identical placeholder presets: masculine and feminine presentations across the same four skin tones.
- Neutral placeholder proportions until final character art is approved with the city art direction.
- A worn off-white starter top and bottom represented as replaceable equipment.

## Guardrails

- Presentation and skin tone are cosmetic only and never change ability, wealth, intelligence, behavior, or athlete outcomes.
- The eight presets are the minimum beta onboarding range, not a deep character creator.
- Placeholder colors, silhouettes, and labels are noncanonical art and may be replaced without changing preset identity or player progress.
- Later appearance editing remains open and is not required to start the beta onboarding path.
