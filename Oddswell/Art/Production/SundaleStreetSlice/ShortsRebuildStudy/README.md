# Upper-shorts rebuild baseline

This study inspects the unchanged [accepted SignalJoin scene](../SignalJoinCandidate/README.md), SHA-256 `440A054D60821E8978AEFC6BB863624766C3FDE0A73CB30D691DD79DDAA4C8C8`. It does not replace the accepted Blender scene. The [front](Upper_Shorts_Front.png), [rear](Upper_Shorts_Rear.png), and [profile](Upper_Shorts_Profile.png) native Cycles renders show the remaining inflated upper shorts, narrow vertical crotch aperture, and simplified seat. They were rendered with [render_baseline.py](render_baseline.py).

The connected shorts mesh has 7,198 controls: seventeen 80-point upper rings, 39 paired gusset stations, and two leg tubes. The visible leg skin stops at Z = 0.706 m; the hidden torso support begins at Z = 0.827 m. Thus the current scene lacks a posed pelvic surface under roughly 121 mm of the upper garment. [measure_scene.py](measure_scene.py) records the current controls; [project_bridge.py](project_bridge.py) locates the saddle in the close cameras. [measure_pose_support.py](measure_pose_support.py) samples a provisional walking-pose pelvis from the existing licensed `source-assets/makehuman-base.obj`, without adding an external asset or modifying the scene. That provisional surface is diagnostic and is not yet registered tightly enough to drive a final garment fit.

Two editable scratch studies lowered the saddle by 28 mm and 16 mm. Both reduced the visible slit, but a local evaluated-surface BVH audit found more nonadjacent cloth overlap pairs than in the accepted scene: 1,290 baseline, 4,354 at 28 mm, and 3,190 at 16 mm. A separate 25 mm rear-seat pull opened a gap under the jacket and scarcely changed the profile. These trials were rejected. The 28 mm study passed the general cold scene check and sampled leg-skin clearance, illustrating why those checks alone cannot establish plausible cloth drape.

The next model needs a pelvis aligned to the saved stance and a new front/rear upper-shorts pattern with a connected crotch and controlled ease, then front/rear/profile/full-body/gameplay native renders. Check the three garment openings, hem and pocket contact, leg-skin clearance, and cloth self-overlap before promotion. The human-realism goal remains active; the current character is visibly CG beside the original photographic concept.
