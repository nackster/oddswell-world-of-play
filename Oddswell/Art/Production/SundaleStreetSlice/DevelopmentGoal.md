## Visual acceptance criteria

- **Character:** credible adult proportions and calf volume; a relaxed walking stance; a slate jacket with shaped shoulders, sleeves, ribbed cuffs and hem, localized garment folds, and a narrow teal yoke sewn onto the surface; cotton shorts with believable fit, hems and pockets; ordinary canvas footwear.
- **Landscape:** mature irregular tree crowns with rounded leaves and branching at several levels; a mixture of shrubs, grasses, flowering stems, groundcover and exposed soil; planted stone seats and paving with small, natural variations.
- **Composition and light:** the complete character reads at the same approximate scale as the reference; a shaded retail promenade opens into a sunlit blue court and arena approach; warm bounced light and varied canopy shadows reveal materials without crushing the foreground into black.
- **OddsWell identity:** slate clothing, restrained teal bands, warm mineral architecture, fired brick, civic planting and the basketball setting form a consistent family recognizable in a screenshot.
- **Proof:** render the gameplay camera and character/landscape inspection views from editable native geometry; inspect for intersections, floating seams, unnatural silhouettes and repeated procedural patterns; retain the source, asset licenses and exact delivery hashes.
