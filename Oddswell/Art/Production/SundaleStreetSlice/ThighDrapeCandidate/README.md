# Anatomical cotton thigh fit

This editable native study continues `CottonTailoringCandidate/CottonDetail.blend` (SHA-256 `9ebfbc9007d4752a568c094d988f36ee9c1fbe342f27f683d7d29d5aa24b8d6f`). It fits the medial cotton panels once against their own anatomical leg in the armature rest position, then lets the retained native walk carry the shape. One added shape key preserves the original controls, topology, UVs and old keys. The registered garment midsurface shares the derived mesh; the hidden historical walk cage retains its original mesh and weights.

Graded central saddle weights transfer opposing leg influence into the pelvis while preserving total bone influence per control. The lower constraint reaches the hem, with separate medial and lateral stand-off. Local smoothing rounds the sharp outer-thigh transition and gives a diagnosed tight inner fold room for the retained 1 mm normal-extruded fabric shell. Sewn pockets, stitches, fly details and turned hems follow the updated cloth atlas. This is authored cloth fitting and deformation, not a physical walking cloth simulation.

Earlier static-looking trials failed actual cloth/body or cloth/self intersection checks. Dynamic nearest-surface projection trials were discarded. The retained fit uses no post-subdivision projection or split-leg target objects. The largest rest-fit control motion is reported in `build-report.json`; this is an actual panel fit, not just a small wrinkle shader adjustment.

Run `build.py`, `audit.py` and `render.py` with Blender 4.5.5 LTS and `--python-exit-code 1 --python`. `render.py -- --close` renders the three garment close-ups; `-- --preview` renders 24 frames for a two-second 12 fps preview. Rendering reuses the native camera helper in `HandArticulationCandidate/render.py`. The cold audit reuses custody/evaluated-mesh helpers from `HipSeatMotionCandidate/audit.py`.

The audit compares all original mesh/curve controls, edges, UVs, old keys, 31 packed maps, 46 native rest matrices and six full-rig poses. It checks the complete evaluated shorts shell against the actual closed anatomical legs and nonadjacent cloth faces across all 49 authored integer frames at render subdivision, plus the validity, stretch and sampled cloth distance of ten visible sewn attachments. See `cold-audit.json` for exact results and scope. The test does not cover the entire upper pelvic anatomy, animated subframes, every accessory, a physical simulation or runtime geometry. Signed nearest-normal distances above an open skin boundary are not used as a clearance certificate.

Licensed source maps remain packed and unchanged. No new downloaded or generated bitmap assets are introduced. The native source chain and its license records remain authoritative. `WholeFigureReview.md` records visible limitations against the original OddsWell reference. This is an offline development checkpoint; the full human/landscape goal remains active, and runtime and beta gates retain their existing status.
