# Registered pelvic fitting support

`PelvicSupport.blend` starts from the exact [SignalJoin checkpoint](../SignalJoinCandidate/README.md), SHA-256 `440A054D60821E8978AEFC6BB863624766C3FDE0A73CB30D691DD79DDAA4C8C8`. The saved scene SHA-256 is `12D2A7382DBDAE6CDDE20D79735E07BBB4F3A7143C2E61CD44605BE1DEAB2432`. [build.py](build.py) extracts one connected 530-control pelvic mesh from the existing licensed `source-assets/makehuman-base.obj`, retains the source vertex IDs, applies the saved walking-pose shift, fits its lower edge to the evaluated anatomical legs, and blends its upper edge toward the existing hidden torso support. The [source license record](../source-assets/MAKEHUMAN-LICENSE.ASSETS.md) is unchanged.

The mesh is hidden in normal renders and marked as an **offline shorts-fitting support**, not a replacement visible body or runtime avatar. [Rear](Pelvic_Support_Rear.png), [front](Pelvic_Support_Front.png), and [profile](Pelvic_Support_Profile.png) are diagnostic renders made with the shorts and jacket hidden; the open cut edges and material difference in those views are intentional evidence that this is an internal fitting surface. They are not finished anatomy renders.

The [fit audit](audit.py) confirms one connected support surface. At Z = 0.66–0.68 m, its lower left and right X/Y bounds are within roughly 6 mm of the saved exposed-leg bounds; through Z = 0.62–0.66 m, sampled surface distance to those legs has a 0.08 mm median and 1.59 mm maximum. At Z = 0.90–0.93 m, its upper controls are within 2.77 mm of the existing torso support. The cold `verify_scene.py --draft` check passes on the saved Blend, and the visible character, garments, original source maps, and game files remain unchanged.

The original [upper-shorts study](../ShortsRebuildStudy/README.md) still applies. This support establishes a consistent body to pattern around; the visible upper shorts still have a broad procedural seat and a narrow crotch aperture. The next art pass must replace or substantially reconstruct the shorts front/rear and crotch around this posed support, with collision-aware drape and native close/full/gameplay review. The human-realism goal remains active.
