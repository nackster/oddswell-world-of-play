# Pelvic cotton fit checkpoint

`PelvicCotton.blend` is an editable native Blender continuation of the [registered pelvic-support scene](../PelvicSupportCandidate/README.md), whose saved SHA-256 is `12D2A7382DBDAE6CDDE20D79735E07BBB4F3A7143C2E61CD44605BE1DEAB2432`. The cotton checkpoint SHA-256 is `CF4ECCDDA1AEA696C61D588B132157727FA3CF6EEB3E1152B681B061BD0FBB87`. The [builder](build.py) verifies its input hash before changing it. The hidden anatomical support and [MakeHuman source/license record](../source-assets/MAKEHUMAN-LICENSE.ASSETS.md) remain in the native file; no new source asset was introduced.

The builder settles the connected shorts for 45 frames against the fitted pelvic support and anatomical legs, pins the waist and hem, retains 35% of the simulated seat movement, and relaxes the strongest folds. It then refits both sewn back pockets and their stitching to keep the original surface clearance. A 4 mm inward correction at the middle of the paired crotch bridge reduces the narrow front/rear opening, fading into the leg pattern. The jacket, body anatomy, face, shoes, and street are unchanged.

[Rear](PelvicCotton_Rear.png), [front](PelvicCotton_Front.png), [profile](PelvicCotton_Profile.png), [full-body rear](PelvicCotton_FullRear.png), and [gameplay](PelvicCotton_Gameplay.png) are native Cycles reviews of the saved candidate, made with [render.py](render.py). A staged study and this one-pass builder agree within 0.003 mm on the shorts controls and sewn pocket geometry.

[audit.py](audit.py) finds 767 nonadjacent overlap pairs in the sampled central cloth region, down from 1,290 in the source. This is a local screening measure, **not** proof of an intersection-free garment. Its pocket-gap comparison matches the source to under 0.1 mm. The saved file also passes `verify_scene.py --draft` (including one connected shorts surface and three intended openings) and the lower-body gait/hem audit. No runtime character integration is claimed.

[The upper-fit ray study](measure_upper_fit.py) identifies why this remains interim: at Z = 0.82 m, median rear clearance from the posed support improves from 23.2 to 19.3 mm, while median front signed clearance improves from -15.6 to -9.3 mm. Seventeen of 27 front rays at that height still have negative clearance. These are sampled front/rear ray distances, not a complete collision test, but they show the existing front pattern can pass through the hidden body support even when the visible scene check passes.

This is an intermediate fit checkpoint, not a finished photoreal human. The small inner-seam crease remains visible close up; the upper shorts still read too broad and smooth, and the jacket and overall character remain visibly CG against the [original street concept](../../../Reviews/OddsWellStreetIdentityCandidate/OddsWell_Visual_Direction_Concept.png). A stronger cloth settle created pinched folds; widening the bridge enlarged the visible gap; an 8 mm inward move hid it but raised sampled overlap pairs to 1,863. The next art pass should rebuild the upper shorts front/rear pattern over the posed support, with controlled crotch ease and seam construction, before further jacket/body and street realism work.
