# Native walking body study

This editable Blender continuation of [CottonTensionCandidate](../CottonTensionCandidate/README.md) develops coordinated adult body motion rather than another static stance. [NativeWalk.blend](NativeWalk.blend) contains fourteen native bones and 49 authored frames at 24 fps: pelvis/chest counter-rotation, lateral weight transfer, bent knees, toe-off and relaxed opposing arm motion. Limb lengths stay fixed. The two-step cycle travels about 0.843 m along the street; the retained two-cycle action travels forward, rather than looping in place.

The anatomical support uses Blender bone-heat binding. Native nearest-polygon weight transfer couples the jacket to that anatomy. Sewn trim and pocket/hem details use native Surface Deform bindings on two triangulated garment cages. Original curves remain editable behind their evaluated mesh followers. The teal signal shader now reads a derived rest-coordinate attribute, so it remains on the jacket during movement. Original mesh/curve control data, original UVs and photographic map bytes remain separately retained.

Review [the walking preview](NativeWalking.mp4), [rear passing pose](Passing_Rear.png), [front passing pose](Passing_Front.png), [torso front](Torso_Front.png), [hips rear](Hips_Rear.png), [contact profile](Contact_Profile.png), [passing profile](Passing_Profile.png), [opposite contact](OppositeContact_Profile.png), and [gameplay composition](Gameplay.png). These are native Cycles renders of the actual geometry. The preview uses 24 rendered frames at 12 fps over two seconds.

This is a development study, not final human realism or a production animation rig. The torso and pelvis carry a coordinated step, but shoulder/underarm folds, hand gesture, shoe finish and facial individuality still read as synthetic in close views. Cloth folds are the retained authored cotton drape deformed with the body. A separate moving-cloth trial did not meet contact/correction limits and is not part of this delivered scene. No runtime movement authority or accepted engine animation is changed.

## Reproduction and evidence

Use Blender 4.5.5 LTS. Run `build.py`, `heat_bind.py`, `bind_surfaces.py` and `bind_signal.py` sequentially with `--python-exit-code 1 --python`; then run `audit.py` and `render.py`. `render.py -- --preview` generates the numbered native preview frames. `landmarks.json` contains measured source joints and sole contact; `inspect.py` can regenerate it from exact source SHA-256 `F9A29E8B87D4F3E7B550C6E0217C2DFE9B80770CD803023E444E5AB74B91046D`. No new asset or texture is downloaded.

The cold-motion audit compares all 4,720 original mesh/curve control sets, original mesh UVs and shape-key coordinates, and all 31 packed source maps. It measures evaluated soles and sampled cloth/body signed clearance through eleven poses. Its nearest-surface screen is a diagnostic, not a complete self-intersection or cloth collision proof. Historical held-attachment samples already negative in the source are reported explicitly rather than counted as new successful contact. The file does not certify every animated subframe, alternate restoration blend, or runtime compatibility. Source curve followers are generated at the retained stance; regenerate them if changing the source curve construction or stance blend.

[Delivery-manifest.json](Delivery-manifest.json) binds delivered source, scripts, reports, renders and preview to their actual hashes. [WholeFigureReview.md](WholeFigureReview.md) records visual limitations and roadmap continuation.
