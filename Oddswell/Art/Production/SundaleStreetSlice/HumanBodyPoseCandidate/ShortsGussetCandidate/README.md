# Shorts gusset development candidate

This editable Blender variant tests a real change to the shorts saddle. It starts from the verified posed character Blend (SHA-256 `001FB31C1C206A4C72245873E44850718CCC5F70CB2D699888FB0E28F727DBEA`) and replaces only the shorts control mesh, then refits its hem and pocket-seam curves. The original retained street-slice Blend remains SHA-256 `0F3988395DBE747A66738D9D7BA1D86A14D2531FCF2A5592C471A01B3AFC6127`.

The locally authored [mesh data](gusset_mesh.json.gz) came from an isolated build of the same native scene with a 44 mm tapered crotch gusset between the two leg panels. The original `t**4` hip-to-leg transition and 40-frame cotton study were retained. The alternate mesh has 7,198 vertices and 7,080 polygons, adding 39 controls and 40 faces to the previous shared center-line saddle. An earlier `t**2` transition produced a sharp rear peak and was rejected. The isolated build also differed in unrelated hand-detail attributes; the whole rebuilt scene was not promoted. [build_gusset_candidate.py](build_gusset_candidate.py) instead fits only the new shorts into the verified posed scene, preserving its other body and garment data.

The four native Cycles renders show close and full-body front/rear views. The rear crotch is rounder than the center-line version, but the improvement is modest. The upper front remains broad and smooth, the left inseam still has a concentrated fold, and the cloth does not yet match the first OddsWell concept. This candidate is not final human art or an animated/runtime asset.

A fresh cold reopen passed with 31 packed maps, ten cameras, one connected shorts surface and the same three openings. Sampled shorts-to-thigh clearance is 2.30 mm, down from 3.50 mm in the posed parent; 668 controls were corrected during the isolated anatomical fit. The right stance sole remains at 51 mm. These are geometry checks for this static pose, not proof of motion compatibility or visual realism. The next pass should refine the gusset curvature, seat volume and leg-panel cloth behavior while preserving more thigh margin, then redo close/full-body visual review before adding pocket or fly construction.
