# Gameplay-scale human and jacket review — September 23, 2026

[Latest native gameplay render](Shorts_Sculpt_Gameplay.png) comes from the exact shorts-sculpt Blender candidate, SHA-256 `7280891086D7F947D33305C640D97A2B464F7602B2E585AB1BDF01248BE697C7`. `render_gameplay_review.py` reproduces it with the saved gameplay camera at 1600 × 900 and 48 Cycles samples. Compare with the [original visual direction](../../../../Reviews/OddsWellStreetIdentityCandidate/OddsWell_Visual_Direction_Concept.png), whose figure is likewise viewed from behind at walking distance.

At this scale the retained jacket reads as a broad, nearly flat slate back with rounded upper sleeves and weak elbow and waist compression. The teal line is recognizable, but its right shoulder junction has a visible tab. The hand gesture and seat/leg-panel cloth remain simplified. The foreground street also lacks the reference's material and architectural density, but the character itself does not yet meet the human-realism gate.

Three isolated jacket studies were rejected. Two direct vertex-normal sculpts (roughly 7–16 mm peak displacement) made only faint back ripples under the current lighting. A 60-frame native cloth resimulation with lower stiffness produced a 56 mm maximum raw motion and about 33 mm maximum difference from the retained simulation. Blending that difference into the saved figure added slight sleeve variation, but the back remained broad at gameplay scale and a cold reopen rejected a detached or buried raglan stitch. None of these studies changed the retained candidate.

Next model actual front/back and raglan sleeve panel shapes around the current weight-bearing pose, with shoulder and elbow ease controlled in the pattern. Settle those connected panels against the anatomical support, then refit the teal yoke, raglan stitches and cuffs to the final cloth surface. Review close rear, full rear and gameplay renders together; require seam contact and positive support clearance before retaining a new candidate. More arbitrary surface ridges are unlikely to solve this scale of shape and drape problem. This review is evidence for the active art goal, not final visual acceptance or runtime integration.
