# Continuous OddsWell signal-stripe candidate

This separate editable scene starts from the exact [directional-lighting candidate](../README.md), SHA-256 `E440EA5F632724BFD584E78E1749CBD6ACB896A22DF6137264E418D70021AE41`. Run `build_signal_stripe_candidate.py` with Blender 4.5 to reproduce `OddsWell_Signal_Stripe_Candidate.blend`, SHA-256 `5E1875679FD06888374E420E05C038B5BD647ED00EA316AC5B36BEB140E81585`. The original scene and character geometry are unchanged.

The torn-looking teal sleeve patches are hidden in viewport and render, but retained in the editable source for provenance. A copy of the slate jacket material now carries one teal signal band across the connected back and sleeves. Its shader uses world-position masks corrected for the jacket's 84.3 mm object-space vertical offset; the rear band curves down toward each shoulder, meeting the sleeve band without a separate floating decal. The existing slate weave color variation and bump remain beneath the teal color. This is a fabric-color design study, not a claim that the stripe has been physically sewn into a new jacket pattern.

The four native 64-sample Cycles reviews are [profile](Signal_Stripe_Profile.png), [full rear](Signal_Stripe_Full_Rear.png), [full front](Signal_Stripe_Full_Front.png), and [1600 × 900 gameplay](Signal_Stripe_Gameplay.png). Profile and rear views now read as a continuous OddsWell cue; the front stays mostly slate. A cold reopen passes the existing static scene check with 31 packed textures, ten cameras, connected jacket and shorts, three shorts openings, 2.48 mm sampled shorts-to-thigh clearance, 4.55 mm free-jacket-to-support clearance, and the planted right sole at 51 mm. No runtime integration is claimed.

This candidate fixes a brand-graphic defect, not the human-realism goal. The jacket shoulder and sleeves remain broad and tube-like, hands still hang stiffly, the shorts need better weight and contact folds, and the full front remains too mannequin-like. Next reshape the connected jacket around a joint-aware walking pose, preserve the continuous signal through that deformation, and review face, hand, cloth and footwear close-ups alongside the full scene before any final visual acceptance.

A later [short-crop and fitted-collar candidate](ShortCropCollarCandidate/README.md) improves the head silhouette and closes the visible neckline gap without changing this checkpoint.
