# Sundale directional-lighting candidate

This separate editable Blender scene starts from the exact [shorts-sculpt human candidate](../ShortsSculptCandidate/README.md), SHA-256 `7280891086D7F947D33305C640D97A2B464F7602B2E585AB1BDF01248BE697C7`. `build_lighting_candidate.py` changes only three daylight fill controls: packed-sky strength from 0.8 to 0.55, broad warm bounce from 700 to 250 W, and courtyard bounce from 2,100 to 700 W. The 6 W warm sun, all geometry, textures, character, camera and source asset licensing remain unchanged. The saved scene is `Sundale_Directional_Lighting_Candidate.blend`, SHA-256 `E440EA5F632724BFD584E78E1749CBD6ACB896A22DF6137264E418D70021AE41`.

The native 64-sample [gameplay](Sundale_Directional_Gameplay.png) and [full rear](Sundale_Directional_Full_Rear.png) Cycles renders show stronger canopy and pavement shadows, a warmer street and a clearer contrast between sunlit court and shaded retail side. A darker test (sky 0.4, bounce 100/300 W) crushed too much of the storefront and was rejected. The retained variant is a lighting direction, not an accepted match to the first [OddsWell concept](../../../../Reviews/OddsWellStreetIdentityCandidate/OddsWell_Visual_Direction_Concept.png); the retail, architecture and planting still need substantial development.

This lighting also exposes the character gap. The jacket back and rounded sleeves still look broad and stiff, and the shorts and hand gestures remain simplified. A separate loose back-panel cloth overlay bunched sharply across the teal yoke. Two connected-shell back-ease simulations produced either a lower-back knot or only a faint, diffuse fold, and the latter failed raglan stitch contact on cold reopen. None of those garment trials was promoted to this scene. The next human pass must rebuild the jacket panel and sleeve fit around the walking pose, then refit the teal yoke, raglan seam and cuffs and inspect close, full and gameplay views. No runtime integration is claimed.

The [profile review](HumanProfileReview.md) records a later native profile render and isolated arm/stripe studies. It confirms that the sleeve stripe is already torn-looking in this retained scene; naive panel projection produces a clean side view but breaks or doubles the stripe from behind. The retained Blender scene stays unchanged while the connected jacket pattern and joint-aware pose are redesigned.

A fresh cold reopen of this lighting scene passes with 31 packed maps, ten cameras, connected jacket and shorts, three shorts openings, 2.48 mm sampled shorts-to-thigh clearance, 4.55 mm free-jacket-to-support clearance and the planted right sole at 51 mm. These static checks do not establish photographic realism, cloth motion compatibility or final scene acceptance.
