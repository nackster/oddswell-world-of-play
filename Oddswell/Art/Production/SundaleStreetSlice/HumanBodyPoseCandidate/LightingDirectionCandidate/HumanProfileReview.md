# Human profile and shoulder-stripe review — September 23, 2026

[Native profile render](Sundale_Human_Profile_Review.png) was made from the unchanged [directional-lighting scene](Sundale_Directional_Lighting_Candidate.blend), SHA-256 `E440EA5F632724BFD584E78E1749CBD6ACB896A22DF6137264E418D70021AE41`. The temporary camera was at `(-2.55, -2.4, 1.25)`, aimed at `(-0.75, -2.4, 1.0)` with a 55 mm lens. The 1000 × 1100 Cycles image used 32 samples. No camera or mesh change was saved into the scene.

The profile exposes three visual blockers that the rear gameplay view understates: a torn-looking teal patch on the upper sleeve, an almost cylindrical uncompressed sleeve and torso, and fingers that hang in a deliberate but stiff gesture. The stripe break exists in the retained scene, rather than being caused by a new arm pose.

Isolated studies stayed in ignored scratch files. An asymmetric world-space arm bend improved the walking gesture from behind but enlarged the tubular sleeve in profile and failed the anatomical hand-relief cold check. Further subdividing and moving the teal sleeve panels outward cleaned one tiny sliver while leaving a broken lower edge. Rebuilding both sleeve bands by ray-casting onto the connected jacket gave a clean profile, and its cold check passed, but the full rear render showed a gap at the rear-yoke join. Extending those bands toward the back closed the profile gap but doubled the stripe over both shoulders in the full rear render. Neither band version is a visual improvement across both views; none was promoted.

The next character build should derive the teal stripe directly from the final connected jacket surface, with its rear and sleeve portions designed as one continuous pattern and seams placed at the raglan join. Refit that pattern after reshaping the shoulder and elbow ease; then pose arms and hands through joint-consistent deformation so the finger shape-key relief and attached nail details stay intact. Review profile, full rear and gameplay renders together before promoting another scene. Passing the existing static geometry checks remains necessary, but does not establish realism.
