The owner requested a more defined human torso and less rigid hips. This study gives the pelvis a graded -4.5 degree yaw, 2.5 degree obliquity and 20 mm stance-side shift, with a separate +3.5 degree chest counter-rotation. The movement blends through the upper thighs, waist and shoulders while ending before the feet and head. A broad lower-rib/waist taper and restrained anterior torso shaping reduce the jacket's cylindrical form. A coupled hip contour reduces the fullest lateral/depth proportions by up to 8.5%/9%, fading before the hands, hems and upper torso. Anatomy, the hidden cloth supports, both garments, trim, pockets and hand details use the same continuous deformation field.

The native empty `Body flow | stance blend` exposes a `flow` property: `0` restores the previous stance and `1` shows this study. It drives `Previous torso hip stance` shape keys on 244 meshes and curves. Original mesh positions are also retained in `Before torso hip flow`; jacket tailoring, collision fit and gravity attributes have their own retained originals. This is an editable static stance control, not a production skeleton or full locomotion cycle.
