The change is restrained at gameplay scale. It does not meet the owner's very-real human target. Further construction and drape work must be judged against the reference, rather than adding more isolated wrinkle marks. The shoes still read as pale smooth shells in those views. Both need direct native modeling/material review in the continuing human work.
