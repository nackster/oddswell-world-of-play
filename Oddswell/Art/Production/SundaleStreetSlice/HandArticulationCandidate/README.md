# Articulated hands and fitted cuffs

This editable native Blender continuation of [ShoulderSurfaceCandidate](../ShoulderSurfaceCandidate/README.md) adds restrained palm and finger movement to the retained adult walk and fits the knitted cuffs to the wrists and actual jacket openings. [HandMotion.blend](HandMotion.blend) retains 46 native bones: the original fourteen body controls plus two palms and three phalange controls for each of ten digits. Existing body rest matrices and eleven checked body poses remain unchanged. This is an offline development checkpoint; the human-realism goal stays active.

The hand weights use the retained canonical anatomical digit chains and the source unposed-position attribute, with smooth transitions across joints and into the palm. Joint centers are fitted from the source skin correspondence. The authored gesture varies across the retained 49-frame action; it is not a fixed hand pose following a forearm. Twenty nail plates and cuticle folds receive the skin's native nearest-polygon interpolated weights. Original hand controls, UVs, shape-key coordinates, anatomical attributes and photographic maps are retained. Four raglan stitches also use native jacket weights and nearest-surface projection onto the moving jacket, replacing a surface binding that stretched in later walking frames.

Each cuff has one added editable `Knitted cuff wrist fit` shape key. The wrist end is centered and tapered against the actual hand surface; the upper rim follows the jacket's boundary. Its native armature weights blend from the forearm at the wrist into the jacket's surface weights at the sleeve join. The original cuff control coordinates and earlier shape-key coordinates remain intact. The largest fitted control change is recorded in [cuff-report.json](cuff-report.json); it includes the correction of the formerly misaligned upper rim, rather than only wrist tapering.

## Reproduction and evidence

Use Blender 4.5.5 LTS, including its bundled NumPy. Run `build.py`, `fit_cuffs.py`, and `stabilize_stitches.py` sequentially, then `stitch_audit.py`, `contact_audit.py`, `audit.py`, `render_hands.py` and `render.py`, using `--python-exit-code 1 --python`. `render.py -- --preview` generates the numbered preview frames. The starting source is `ShoulderSurfaceCandidate/RoundedShoulder.blend`, SHA-256 `7e04b1847acdbb58096ff57c654e388768e91726808f95caaf203e8ef18f82dd`. Builders locate the repository root and save outputs in this folder. No external asset or texture is downloaded.

The fresh cold geometry audit compares original mesh/curve controls, original UVs and protected shape-key coordinates, and all 31 packed source maps. It permits only the two explicitly added cuff keys. Eleven poses retain positive sampled free-jacket clearance, fixed bone scales and measured sole contact. The hand contact audit separately checks the original fourteen body rest/pose matrices, actual fingertip motion relative to the forearm, sampled nail/fold proximity to skin, finite positive cuff triangle areas and bounded cuff edge lengths. These checks do not certify every animated subframe, complete hand/cloth self-intersection freedom, alternate restoration blends, or runtime compatibility. The eight negative held-jacket samples inherited from the source remain reported. Small embedded cuticle samples are disclosed rather than described as zero-intersection contact.

Automatic bone-heat hand binding, including a remeshed proxy trial, failed and was rejected. A cuff Surface Deform trial stretched the parented cuff during movement and was also rejected; the delivered cuffs use the shared armature blend. Later preview frames exposed stretched raglan stitching in the inherited surface binding; those four stitches now use stable armature weights and surface projection, with edge-length and sampled jacket-contact checks over all 49 authored integer frames. The rejected cuff and raglan bindings are absent from the delivered scene. The retained authored cotton drape and outside-body jacket constraint are not a moving cloth simulation.
