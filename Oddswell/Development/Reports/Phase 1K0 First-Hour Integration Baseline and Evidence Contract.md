## Evidence discipline

The canonical contract requires identity, UTC and monotonic time, ordered actions, exact prompts, confusion, every intervention, every QA flag, hashes, full ledger links, peer observations, cold-process boundaries, and a no-hidden-data record. All post-setup developer intervention counts. An attempt with assistance is retained as failed rather than silently repaired.

The contract rejects spliced evidence, a watched/settled result mismatch, hidden/direct QA actions, teleports, partial authority, mismatched hashes, unexpected money mutation, active peer assistance, false cold-equip persistence, hidden simulator/athlete values, and leftover processes.
