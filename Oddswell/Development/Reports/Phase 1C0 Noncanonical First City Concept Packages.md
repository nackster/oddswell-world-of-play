# Phase 1C.0 — Noncanonical First City Concept Packages

Date: 2026-07-20

## Outcome

Phase 1C.0 produced three visual, planning-only first-city alternatives for owner comparison: Mainline/Rookwell, Daymark/Sundale, and Northreach/Alderpoint. All three follow the owner's direction that the beta city should be modern-day, more distinctive than a plain generic city, and less elaborate than later water-focused or Tartaria-inspired cities.

No alternative is canon. Phase 1C remains at its owner gate and no Unreal map, source, configuration, runtime, production art, 3D asset, multiplayer, apartment, economy, wagering, deployment, or brain behavior changed.

## Bible and roadmap coverage

- Advances planning evidence for `Enter the first city`, `Required beta locations`, and `Art, music, and storytelling`.
- Covers the owner-gate fields: proposed world/city name, theme, geography/core loop, visual language, and a minimal art-direction sheet.
- Preserves one compact city, all seven required beta location types, third-person walkability, modern-day scale, and environmental storytelling.
- Does not satisfy the Phase 1C exit gate; no selected direction or measured graybox route exists.

## Generated-image provenance

The three images were generated with Codex's built-in image-generation tool from separate `stylized-concept` game-environment prompts. Each prompt required an original fictional, modern-day, human-scale city; one small third-person player in worn off-white starter clothing; a compact route containing or implying the required beta locations; believable present-day materials; no readable text, logos, trademarks, real brands, watermark, sci-fi, fantasy, canals, water-city features, Tartarian monumentality, mansions, or extravagant luxury.

Package-specific prompt direction:

- **Rookwell:** warm brick renewal district, steel viaduct, court, shops, arena beacon, wet early evening.
- **Sundale:** pale concrete and terracotta commons, shade trees, court, shops, arena axis, warm late afternoon.
- **Alderpoint:** dark brick and concrete terraces, step-free ramps, planted walls, court, upper-slope arena, cool blue hour.

The outputs are concept images only. They are not licensed production assets, approved final art, 3D geometry, material references, level measurements, or proof that Unreal can reproduce the scene. Any selected direction still requires source/license review and deliberate asset production.
